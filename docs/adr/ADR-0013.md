# ADR-0013 — Univents on Cloudflare: delayed jobs enqueued inside the transaction

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Scope:** `lib/go/jobs`, `api/univents`, `justfile`, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`

## Context

Univents is the last Backend to move to the ADR-0011 runtime, and the last
River user. Its jobs differ from Payssage's: the purchase expiry is
scheduled 10:01 ahead inside the checkout transaction (`river.InsertTx`
with `ScheduledAt`) and cancelled on approval (`JobCancel`); the gift email
is enqueued inside the transaction that confirms the registration; the two
certificate jobs are plain enqueues. A Cloudflare Queue cannot cancel a
message once sent, and `lib/jobs` had no delay.

## Decision

- **`lib/jobs` gains delayed enqueue.** `DelayedEnqueuer.EnqueueAfter`
  (max 12h, the Queues limit): the in-process runner holds a timer;
  `httpjobs` sends the delay in `Job-Delay-Seconds`, and the Worker passes
  it to the queue as the message delay.
- **Enqueue inside the transaction, before commit, with a delay.** The
  expiry job is delayed to `expires_at`, the gift email by 30s. The delay
  means the job cannot run before the commit lands; a failed expiry enqueue
  fails the checkout, as before; a rolled-back transaction leaves a job
  that finds no row (or an unconfirmed one) and no-ops. No sweep, no
  migration, no lost-enqueue window.
- **Approval no longer cancels the expiry.** The expiry worker already
  no-ops on a purchase that is not pending, so the delayed message arriving
  after approval does nothing — the same outcome as the cancel, for the
  buyer. `purchases.river_job_id` is left in place, unused.
- **Retries belong to the queue** (30s doubling, capped at an hour,
  `max_retries` 10); exhausted jobs stay on `univents-jobs-dlq`.
- **Realtime is off on Cloudflare.** The WS hub and SSE relay subscribe via
  Postgres LISTEN, which Neon's pooled endpoint does not support. Left as
  is; realtime will be redesigned separately.
- The rest follows ADR-0011: River, its migrator and dashboard are gone,
  and the schema is migrated outside the process (`just univents-goose`).

## Consequences

- A job enqueued for a transaction that then rolls back still runs once,
  as a no-op.
- No Backend uses `lib/go/river` any more; it can be deleted with its
  dependencies, and the River tables dropped, in a follow-up.
