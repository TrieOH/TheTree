## Why

Univents is the last backend to move to the ADR-0011 setup, and the last one on River. Its jobs aren't like Payssage's:

- The purchase expiry is scheduled 10:01 ahead inside the checkout transaction (`river.InsertTx` with `ScheduledAt`) and cancelled when the payment is approved (`JobCancel`).
- The gift email is enqueued inside the transaction that confirms the registration.
- The two certificate jobs are plain enqueues.

A Cloudflare Queue can't cancel a message once it's sent, and `lib/jobs` had no way to delay a job.

## Decision

- **`lib/jobs` gains delayed jobs.** `DelayedEnqueuer.EnqueueAfter`, up to 12 hours, the Queues limit. The in-process runner holds a timer. `httpjobs` sends the delay in `Job-Delay-Seconds`, and the Worker passes it to the queue as the message delay.
- **Enqueue inside the transaction, before commit, with a delay.** The expiry job is delayed until `expires_at`, the gift email by 30 seconds. The delay means the job can't run before the commit lands. A failed expiry enqueue fails the checkout, as before. If the transaction rolls back, the job finds no row, or an unconfirmed one, and does nothing. No sweep, no migration, and no window where an enqueue is lost.
- **Approval no longer cancels the expiry.** The expiry worker already skips a purchase that isn't pending, so a message that arrives after approval does nothing. For the buyer, that's the same as cancelling it. `purchases.river_job_id` stays, unused.
- **The queue owns retries:** 30 seconds, doubling, capped at an hour, up to 10. Jobs that run out stay on `univents-jobs-dlq`.
- **Realtime is off on Cloudflare.** The WebSocket hub and the SSE relay listen through Postgres LISTEN, which Neon's pooled endpoint doesn't support. We left it as is, and realtime will be redesigned separately.
- **Everything else follows ADR-0011.** River, its migrator and its dashboard are gone, and the schema is migrated outside the process (`just univents-goose`).

## Consequences

- A job enqueued by a transaction that rolls back still runs once, and does nothing.
- No backend uses `lib/go/river` any more.
