# ADR-0012 — Payssage on Cloudflare: delivery rows are the outbox, the carrier owns retries

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Scope:** `api/payssage`, `justfile`, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`

## Context

Payssage is the second Backend to move to the ADR-0011 runtime. Its one
background job, webhook delivery, ran on River: `dispatchDeliveries`
inserted each `webhook_deliveries` row and its River job in the same
transaction (`river.InsertTx`), and the worker kept its own retry budget in
`webhook_deliveries.attempts` (5), returning nil to stop River once spent.

`lib/jobs/httpjobs` enqueues with a plain POST, which cannot join a database
transaction, and on Cloudflare the Queue, not the process, decides when a
job runs again.

## Decision

- **The delivery row is the source of truth; the job is a doorbell.**
  Dispatch commits the event's delivery rows in one transaction, then
  enqueues one `webhook.deliver` per row after the commit. A failed enqueue
  is logged, not returned. Enqueueing before the commit is ruled out: a
  worker could look for a row that is not visible yet and give up on it.
- **`webhook.redrive` closes the gap.** A periodic job (every 15 minutes,
  `jobs.Periodic` and the Worker's cron) re-enqueues pending deliveries that
  were never attempted and are older than 2 minutes.
- **The worker is idempotent.** A delivery that is no longer `pending` is a
  no-op, so duplicates from the sweep or from queue retries are harmless.
  Delivery stays at-least-once, as it was with River.
- **The carrier owns retries.** A failed attempt is recorded on the row,
  which stays `pending`, and the job returns an error (5xx at `/events`).
  The Worker retries with per-message backoff (30s doubling, capped at an
  hour, `max_retries` 10). The worker's own attempt budget is gone.
- **The dead-letter queue records the give-up.** The Worker consumes
  `payssage-jobs-dlq` and turns each dead `webhook.deliver` into
  `webhook.fail`, which marks the delivery `failed` and keeps the last
  attempt's response. Locally the in-process runner drops a job after its
  attempts and the row stays `pending`.
- **The rest follows ADR-0011:** River, its dashboard and `SIMPLE_AUTH_*`
  are gone; the schema is migrated outside the process (`just
  payssage-goose`); Cloudflare-hosted services skip the deploy ledger.

## Consequences

- No outbox table: the deliveries table already is one. If another Backend
  needs transactional enqueue for work with no natural row, a shared outbox
  in `lib/jobs` is the next step, built from two real cases.
- A job queued longer than 2 minutes may be enqueued twice by the sweep; the
  idempotent worker makes that a second no-op unless both run concurrently,
  in which case the tenant receives the webhook twice.
- Locally, a delivery whose in-process attempts run out stays `pending`.
- Periodic cadences are declared twice, as in ADR-0011.
